04 · ET view

ET analysis

ET
Byline pendingDraft analysis · Version 1

Credit is the input most tied to policy

Read Rajan for where lending standards are heading. For a small business, that matters more than the next rate move.

A rate cut helps only if a bank will lend. His framework puts weight on clean bank balance sheets. That makes credit sounder over time, but tighter at first for small and unrated borrowers.

Watch: banking-system bad-loan ratios, RBI policy statements, and his commentary on bank health.
