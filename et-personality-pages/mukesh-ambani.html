04 · ET view

ET
Byline pendingDraft analysis · Version 1

The cheapest supplier is also the biggest competitor

When Reliance lowers prices on data, cloud or logistics, small businesses get cheaper inputs. They also face a rival with the same advantages.

The pattern from telecom repeats in each new business: enter at low prices, win scale, then set terms. For a supplier, Reliance can be the largest customer. For a retailer or app maker, it can be the toughest rival.

Watch: pricing of the AI and cloud offers, supplier terms at Reliance Retail, and the Jio listing prospectus.
