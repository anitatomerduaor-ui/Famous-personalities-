Read the tariff line, skip the noise

Most of what he says on a given day will not change your business. The tariff schedule will. Follow that.

For an SME, three questions settle almost every announcement: does it change the duty on my product's tariff line, does it change what my US buyer can pay, and does it change my input costs? If none, it belongs in "Also today", not in your alerts.

Watch: the published tariff schedule, the trade-deal talks, and any sector-specific duty.
