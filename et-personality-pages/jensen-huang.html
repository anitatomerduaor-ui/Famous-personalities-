Your AI bill is set in his keynotes

Each new NVIDIA platform lowers the cost of running AI. That saving reaches small firms last, through the tools they subscribe to.

For a business owner, the useful question is not who wins the chip race. It is when the tools you already pay for get cheaper or better. Large clouds buy first. Software makers repackage the gains. Small businesses see them a year or more later.

Watch: supply announcements at each GTC, cloud price cuts, and any export rule that tightens supply.
